# Video Generation Prompt Guide

## How to Write Effective Prompts

### Best Practices

1. **Be Specific and Descriptive**
   - Good: "Ocean waves crashing on a sandy beach at sunset"
   - Bad: "Beach"

2. **Include Motion or Action**
   - Good: "Cars driving on a busy highway at night"
   - Bad: "Highway"

3. **Add Atmosphere or Mood**
   - Good: "Peaceful forest with sunlight filtering through trees"
   - Bad: "Forest"

4. **Mention Time of Day**
   - Good: "City skyline at golden hour with traffic"
   - Bad: "City"

## Prompt Examples by Category

### Nature & Landscapes
- "Mountain peaks covered in snow with clouds moving"
- "Waterfall flowing in tropical rainforest"
- "Desert landscape with sand dunes at sunset"
- "Northern lights dancing in arctic sky"

### Urban & City
- "Busy city street with people walking and cars"
- "Downtown skyline at night with lights"
- "Traffic flowing on highway at dusk"
- "Urban park with people relaxing"

### Action & Motion
- "Athlete running on track in slow motion"
- "Drone flying over coastal landscape"
- "Surfer riding ocean wave"
- "Skateboarder performing tricks in park"

### Weather & Atmosphere
- "Storm clouds gathering over ocean"
- "Rain falling on city street at night"
- "Fog rolling over mountain valley"
- "Sunset with dramatic clouds"

### Abstract & Concepts
- "Time-lapse of clouds moving across sky"
- "Busy marketplace with people shopping"
- "Construction site with workers and machinery"
- "Technology and innovation in modern office"

## Tips for Better Results

1. **Use 5-15 words** - Not too short, not too long
2. **Include concrete nouns** - Specific objects and places
3. **Add descriptive adjectives** - Beautiful, dramatic, peaceful
4. **Mention movement** - Flowing, moving, driving, walking
5. **Avoid abstract concepts alone** - Combine with concrete visuals

## What Works Best

✅ Natural scenes (ocean, mountains, forests)
✅ Urban environments (cities, streets, traffic)
✅ Weather phenomena (rain, clouds, sunset)
✅ People activities (walking, working, sports)
✅ Transportation (cars, planes, boats)

## What to Avoid

❌ Very specific people or celebrities
❌ Copyrighted characters or brands
❌ Complex multi-scene narratives
❌ Text or written content
❌ Very abstract concepts without visuals

## Example Transformations

### Improve Your Prompts

**Before**: "War"
**After**: "Military vehicles moving through desert with smoke"

**Before**: "Happy"
**After**: "People celebrating and dancing at outdoor festival"

**Before**: "Technology"
**After**: "Modern office with people working on computers"

**Before**: "Nature"
**After**: "Lush green forest with sunlight and flowing stream"

## Generation Time

- Simple prompts: 15-20 seconds
- Complex prompts: 25-35 seconds
- Cached results: 5-10 seconds

## Need Help?

If generation fails:
1. Try simplifying your prompt
2. Use more common words
3. Focus on visual elements
4. Check the examples above
